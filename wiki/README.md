# Taiwan Mandarin Learning Wiki
# 台灣華語學習指南：從 A1 到 C1

Welcome to my Taiwan Mandarin Learning Wiki!

I created this project to help people learn Mandarin Chinese, especially learners who want to understand and use **Taiwan Mandarin** in everyday life.

My goal is to build a structured learning resource from beginner to advanced levels, with clear explanations, practical examples, exercises, and information about Taiwanese culture.

## 1. Project Goals

In this Wiki, I aim to:

- Help learners study Taiwan Mandarin step by step, from beginner to advanced levels.
- Provide explanations in Traditional Chinese and Burmese.
- Include both Zhuyin (注音) and Pinyin (拼音) to help learners understand pronunciation.
- Teach useful vocabulary, grammar, conversations, and everyday expressions.
- Introduce Taiwanese culture and daily life through language learning.
- Provide exercises, quizzes, and self-assessment activities.

## 2. Learning Structure

I organize the Wiki into five learning levels:

| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| A1 | Beginner |
| A2 | Elementary |
| B1 | Intermediate |
| B2 | Upper Intermediate |
| C1 | Advanced |

These levels are used as the internal structure of my project. They are not officially certified equivalents of CEFR, TOCFL, or TBCL levels.

Each level is planned to include six learning sections:

1. **Pronunciation (發音)** – Zhuyin, Pinyin, tones, and pronunciation practice.
2. **Vocabulary (詞彙)** – Common words, expressions, and practical usage.
3. **Grammar (語法)** – Grammar rules, sentence structures, and examples.
4. **Listening & Speaking (聽力與口說)** – Dialogues, listening practice, and speaking activities.
5. **Reading & Writing (閱讀與寫作)** – Reading comprehension, Chinese characters, and writing practice.
6. **Taiwan Culture & Daily Life (台灣文化與日常生活)** – Taiwanese customs, food, transportation, shopping, and daily communication.

## 4. Languages and Pronunciation Systems

My learning materials focus on Taiwan Mandarin and use:

- **Traditional Chinese (繁體中文)** as the main Chinese writing system.
- **Burmese (မြန်မာဘာသာ)** for additional explanations.
- **Pinyin (拼音)** to help learners read Mandarin pronunciation.
- **Zhuyin (注音)** to introduce the pronunciation system commonly used in Taiwan.

Some English translations are also included to make the learning materials easier to understand.

## 5. Learning Activities

Depending on the lesson, I plan to include:

- Pronunciation practice
- Listening, speaking, reading, and writing exercises

The activities will help learners review what they have learned and check their understanding.

## 6. Sources and References

I use learning materials from different sources, including:

Some examples and explanations are my own teaching adaptations. I distinguish these from information directly supported by the original sources.
