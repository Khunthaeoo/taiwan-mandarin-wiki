# A1–C1 evidence coverage matrix

**Scope:** Taiwan Mandarin Learning Guide; five project levels × six sections = 30 cells.  
**Evidence basis:** Existing source register and evidence cards ACCC-01–ACCC-21, read for this audit.

## Status definitions

- **Strong:** Direct, level-specific evidence covers the main skills/content in the cell with adequate traceable locations.
- **Partial:** Relevant source material exists, but it is cross-level, not mapped to this project's level, or lacks important parts of the section.
- **Missing:** No relevant source evidence was found for that cell.

The project's A1/A2/B1/B2/C1 labels are its organizing levels. This matrix does not infer a source-to-project-level mapping from difficulty or numbering. The ACCC series' collective CEFR A1–C1 statement and its Volume 5 approximate CEFR B2–C1 description remain source claims; ACCC-19's TBCL Level 4 claim is not converted to CEFR or to a project level. NAER reports TBCL Level 1 below CEFR A1 in speaking and Levels 1–2 below A1 in listening, reading, and writing (ACCC-20); the first-level prototype (ACCC-21) is a beginner reference, not a project-level mapping. Accordingly, a source may support a section without supporting its placement at a particular level.

No cell is marked **Missing** because the source collection contains at least some section-relevant material for each topic across the collection. All cells remain **Partial** because none is securely assigned to the corresponding project level, and each has a substantial identified gap. This does not satisfy the project gate: partial cross-level evidence is not a substitute for level-specific evidence. The 30-cell evidence coverage gate is **NOT PASSED**.

## Scope and source-family review

- **Counts:** The collection has 21 distinct works/material entries and seven identifiable source families: ACCC; Fulbright/FLTA; Innovative Language Learning/ChineseClass101; MOE; NTU; FICHET/TMERC; and NAER. Six ACCC volumes are one family; both FICHET resources and both NAER resources are each grouped as one. Audio folders are probably companions to ACCC, but attribution remains provisional; the unidentified video is not counted as a family.
- **Independent/reliable coverage:** Six families are institutional or otherwise traceable for language-learning use, but that count is collection-wide, not proof of adequate independent evidence at every level. The commercial Survival Phrases is supplemental. No family supplies a verified project-level syllabus for all five levels.
- **Six sections:** Each has at least some source content somewhere in the collection; pronunciation references and practical/cultural topics are relatively well represented. Vocabulary/grammar are broad but unlevelled. Reading/writing and listening have important progression, transcript, and feedback gaps.
- **Listening:** The Huayu101 pages expose phrase audio; the FICHET *驚豔臺灣* resource links 12 MP3s and Chinese episode text/worksheets. The 734 local MP3s are inventoried but not played, transcribed, or definitively attributed. Identifiable, levelled listening evidence remains insufficient.
- **Taiwan Mandarin fit:** ACCC, Huayu101, and FICHET resources give Taiwan-specific topics and expressions; MOE/NTU pronunciation resources cover standard Mandarin pronunciation. The collection needs more current evidence for colloquial/register variation and Taiwan-specific language choices across levels, without assuming all general-Mandarin content is Taiwan-specific.
- **Language requirement:** No evidence card supplies Burmese explanations. Burmese translation and expert review remain missing across the entire curriculum.
