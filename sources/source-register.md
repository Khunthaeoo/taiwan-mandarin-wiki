# Source register — inspected learning resources

This register covers all six volumes of one textbook series. They are six source documents but one source family, not six independent publishers or corroborating sources.

## ACCC-01 — Volume 1

- **Title:** *A Course in Contemporary Chinese: Textbook, Vol. 1*; PDF metadata title: `當代中文課程1 – 課本`.
- **Level:** Volume 1 (the book does not assign this individual volume to a particular CEFR sublevel in the checked material).
- **Author/editor:** PDF metadata names 鄧守信; the series foreword identifies him as chief editor.
- **Publisher/organization:** 國立臺灣師範大學國語教學中心 (Mandarin Training Center, National Taiwan Normal University) planned the series. The foreword thanks 聯經出版事業股份有限公司 for its publishing/production assistance; a formal imprint was not confirmed.
- **Edition/publication year:** No edition or publication year identified. PDF metadata timestamps are not treated as publication dates.
- **URL:** No source/download URL identified in the checked pages.
- **Original filename:** `A Course in Contemporary Chinese-1 (570w).pdf`
- **Main topics:** Fifteen lessons on introductions, family, weekends, shopping, food, school, entertainment, travel, fruit, housing, study, birthdays, weather, and illness (contents, printed pp. VI–VII; lesson starts pp. 1–323).
- **Wiki sections supported:** Vocabulary, Grammar, Listening & Speaking, Taiwan Culture & Daily Life (strong); Pronunciation and Reading & Writing (partial).
- **Wiki levels supported:** The series introduction says Volumes 1–6 collectively cover CEFR A1–C1 (printed p. V). No individual Volume 1-to-Wiki-level mapping is established.

- **Evidence card:** [ACCC-01](evidence-cards/ACCC-01.md)
