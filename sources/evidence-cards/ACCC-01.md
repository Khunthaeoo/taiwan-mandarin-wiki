# Evidence card ACCC-01 — *A Course in Contemporary Chinese*, Vol. 1

**Source record:** [ACCC-01](../source-register.md)  
**Original file:** `A Course in Contemporary Chinese-1 (570w).pdf` (399 PDF pages)  
**PDF title/author metadata:** `當代中文課程1 – 課本`; author field `鄧守信`. The foreword identifies the Mandarin Training Center at National Taiwan Normal University as the series organization and 鄧守信 as chief editor (printed p. II).  
**Publisher note:** The foreword thanks 聯經出版事業股份有限公司 for publishing/production assistance (printed p. II); the formal imprint is not confirmed. No edition/publication year or source URL was identified in the checked pages.

## Structure and relevant content

The contents list 15 lessons, from personal introductions and family through shopping, food, school, entertainment, travel, housing, birthdays, weather, and illness. Lesson starts run from printed p. 1 to p. 323; contents are on printed pp. VI–VII. The series introduction describes Volume 1 as strengthening daily conversation and applications (printed p. V).

## Wiki-level support

The series introduction states that Volumes 1–6 collectively cover CEFR A1–C1 (printed p. V). This volume can inform the series-wide progression, but the checked source does not map Volume 1 to a specific Wiki level. It gives no TOCFL equivalence.

## Limitations and gaps

- No Burmese explanations were identified; English translations are used.
- Pinyin and general phonology are explicit; systematic Zhuyin presentation and practice are not established in the checked overview.
- Companion workbook, character-practice book, teacher's manual, and audio are not supplied in this PDF.
- The evidence above is selective, based on the contents, introductory pages, lesson highlights, and indexes; it is not a transcription of all lessons.
