# Evidence card ACCC-20 — NAER《臺灣華語文能力基準》簡介

**Source record:** [ACCC-20](../source-register.md)  
**Original URL:** https://coct.naer.edu.tw/page.jsp?ID=10  
**Organization:** National Academy for Educational Research (NAER); the page says the Ministry of Education commissioned NAER to develop the framework.  
**Publication information:** No publication date displayed on the checked page.

## Framework evidence

The page describes the Taiwan Benchmarks for the Chinese Language (TBCL) as seven proficiency levels across listening, speaking, reading, writing, and translation. It also reports lists of 3,100 Chinese characters, 14,425 words, and 496 grammar points for Levels 1–5.

The page's CEFR comparison states that TBCL Levels 1 and 2 are below CEFR A1 in listening, reading, and writing, and TBCL Level 1 is below CEFR A1 in speaking. Thus **TBCL Level 1 is not equivalent to CEFR A1**. The page does not state a corresponding comparison for translation.

## Wiki relevance and limits

This is level-framework context only. It does not directly show, define, or provide Pinyin, Zhuyin, meanings, or usage evidence for any of the seven proposed expressions (你好、再見、謝謝、不客氣、你叫什麼名字？、我姓……，叫……、很高興認識你。). It cannot establish that the complete list is A1 or mapped to the Wiki's internal A1 level.
