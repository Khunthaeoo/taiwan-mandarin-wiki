# Evidence card ACCC-18 — *華語101 / Huayu101*

**Source record:** [ACCC-18](../source-register.md)  
**Original URL:** https://lmit.edu.tw/lc/huayu101/  
**Organization:** Taiwan Mandarin Educational Resources Center (TMERC/FICHET); the page title in the site's HTML is “Huayu101 | Taiwan Mandarin Educational Resources Center.”

## Structure and learning evidence

The landing page lists 12 practical scenarios: greetings, accommodation, ordering, shopping, transportation, asking for help, city tours, arts/cultural activities, calls/reservations, currency exchange, making friends, and hospital/post-office visits. The checked chapter pages present Traditional Chinese phrases with Pinyin and embedded audio controls. Chapter 1 provides greetings; chapter 3 includes ordering, food names, and requests; chapter 12 includes medical and post-office phrases.

## Wiki relevance and limits

Useful Taiwan daily-life vocabulary and listening/speaking prompts. The pages display phrase text and Pinyin, but no continuous dialogue transcripts, structured grammar progression, or CEFR/TBCL level was identified. No Zhuyin or Burmese content. Reading/writing support is incidental to displayed phrase text.

**Reliability / access / rights:** Official center resource; public web pages and embedded audio controls are accessible. No downloadable audio/transcript or explicit reuse license was identified on the checked pages. Public access is not permission to copy illustrations or audio.
