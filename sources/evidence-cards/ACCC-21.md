# Evidence card ACCC-21 — NAER《教材模板 第一級〈問好〉：你是美國人嗎？》

**Publication information:** Eight-page PDF labeled 第一級; no publication date displayed.

## Evidence locations

- **PDF p. 1:** The first-level prototype's opening dialogue directly uses “早安，你好！” and “你好。” Its stated objectives cover asking about nationality, apologizing and responding, family terms, and likes/dislikes.
- **PDF pp. 2–3:** Vocabulary tables provide English meanings, Hanyu Pinyin, and Zhuyin. The p. 2 table includes **早安** (zǎoān; ㄗㄠˇ ㄢ; “Good morning”).
- **PDF p. 5:** A possessive-pattern example asks “你的名字叫安安嗎？” This is a name-related example, not the proposed question “你叫什麼名字？”.
- **PDF p. 7:** An activity asks learners to interview classmates for their Chinese names and nationalities.

## Support for the proposed vocabulary

- **Directly shown:** **你好** appears in the p. 1 dialogue.
- **Related topic, not the proposed expression:** The p. 5 example and p. 7 activity concern names, but do not directly teach **你叫什麼名字？** or **我姓……，叫……**.
- **Not directly shown in this prototype:** **再見、謝謝、不客氣、很高興認識你。**
- **Additional beginner greeting evidence:** The source directly supports **早安**, which is not part of the approved seven-expression list, and provides its English meaning, Pinyin, and Zhuyin.

## Wiki relevance and limits

The material is a useful first-level beginner reference for a greeting dialogue and a name-related activity. It supports **你好** as beginner content, but not the complete seven-expression list or its placement at the Wiki's internal A1 level. Do not equate the source's TBCL level with CEFR A1; see [ACCC-20](ACCC-20.md). Its stated objectives also extend beyond greetings. No Burmese explanations are provided.
