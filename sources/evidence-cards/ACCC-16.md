# Evidence card ACCC-16 — MOE *國語注音符號手冊*

**Source record:** [ACCC-16](../source-register.md)  
**Official resource:** [MOE manual HTML landing page](https://language.moe.gov.tw/001/Upload/files/SITE_CONTENT/M0001/deploy/index.html), with HTML and EPUB reading/download options.  
**Local copy:** `sources/zh.epub` is the same manual and is not a separate source work.

## Bibliographic evidence

The digital edition credits the Ministry of Education (MOE) and its Lifelong Education Department; the print foreword describes a committee of Mandarin phonetics experts, convened by 李鍌. The site gives print first edition November 2000 and digital edition January 2017 (ISBN 978-986-051-481-0). EPUB metadata says `2016-12-31`; this metadata date differs from the webpage's publication month.

## Structure and learning evidence

The manual is a Traditional-Chinese reference, not a levelled course. The contents lead to the complete symbol table and consonant/vowel descriptions; tone-mark descriptions and placement are in the fourth subsection (HTML/printed pp. 4–7). Character-annotation examples are on pp. 8–25; syllable-table examples are on pp. 26–32, organized by 開口呼、齊齒呼、合口呼、撮口呼. The FAQ states that highlighted symbol cells can play sound and stroke-order cells show animation. It standardizes Zhuyin notation and gives sound/tone reference examples; it does not teach Hanyu Pinyin or communicative skills.

## Wiki relevance and limits

Strong primary reference for Zhuyin forms, writing/stroke order, tone-mark conventions, and syllable lookup. It is not a full pronunciation sequence, a practice curriculum, or evidence for a CEFR/TBCL level. No Burmese explanations.
