# Evidence card ACCC-19 — *驚豔臺灣學華語*

**Source record:** [ACCC-19](../source-register.md)  
**Original URL:** https://lmit.edu.tw/zh/tc_reference_detail/30  
**Organization / credits:** Planned by FICHET / Taiwan Mandarin Educational Resources Center; Huayu teachers 王瑋姁 and 李百育; design by 汲思有限公司. Adapted from the center's podcast topics.

## Structure and learning evidence

The page describes a 12-unit resource around Taiwan landscapes, food, performing arts, festivals, temples, and cultural practices. The linked items include Taroko, Taroko trails, Michelin food, Taipei 101, Alishan and its tea/indigenous culture, National Palace Museum, role-play, Taiwanese opera, the Lantern Festival, Dajia Mazu pilgrimage, and temple architecture. The page says the material uses vocabulary at TBCL Level 4; this is not a CEFR or project-level mapping.

The page links full-unit PDFs and a correction sheet; one full-unit PDF URL responded with PDF content. A linked Google Drive folder displayed 12 named MP3 episode files. The related podcast page says Chinese episode text and Chinese/English worksheets are provided. This supports listening and reading with companion study material, plus vocabulary and Taiwan-culture learning.

## Wiki relevance and limits

Strong culture and reading/listening supplement, with some role-play and vocabulary. The resource does not establish a systematic Zhuyin/Pinyin or grammar course, Burmese explanations, or A1–C1 mapping. Verify cultural and factual claims against current primary sources.

**Reliability / access / rights:** Official Taiwan Mandarin learning-center resource with named Huayu teachers; page, linked PDF, and shared audio folder were accessible at inspection. No reuse license was identified. Download availability does not grant permission to reproduce or adapt the PDFs/audio.
