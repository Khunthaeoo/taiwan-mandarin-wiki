**Publisher note:** The foreword thanks 聯經出版事業股份有限公司 for publishing assistance (printed p. II); the formal imprint is not confirmed. The foreword is dated May 4, 2018 (printed p. II), not treated as a verified publication year.

## Structure and relevant content

The contents list 10 lessons (printed pp. VI–VII), from **「職校教育」** (p. 1) to **「智慧與能力」** (p. 221). Other topics include technology, dance, interpersonal conduct, international languages, pandas, relationships, the Olympics, and the meaning of home. The series introduction places Volumes 4–6 in its written-language portion and says Volumes 5–6 use topics such as society, technology, economics, politics, culture, and the environment (printed p. V). The Chinese–English and English–Chinese vocabulary indexes begin at printed pp. 253 and 269; a simplified-character reference begins at p. 291 (contents, p. VII).

## Wiki-level support

The series introduction states that Volumes 1–6 collectively cover CEFR A1–C1 (printed p. V), but it does not assign Volume 6 a specific CEFR sublevel or map it to the project's five Wiki levels. No TOCFL equivalence is stated.

## Limitations and gaps

- Explanations use English, not Burmese.
- Pinyin, sounds, and tones are discussed in the introduction, but systematic Zhuyin teaching and practice are not established.
- The separate student workbook and teacher's manual are not included; their listening materials and answer support were not checked.
- This evidence card summarizes the contents, lesson highlights, introductory pages, and selected early pages; it is not a transcription or full lesson-by-lesson review.
- Several topics concern cross-cultural or broad social issues; check claims and source citations in the original readings before using them as current Taiwan facts.
