## Structure and relevant content

The contents list 12 lessons (printed pp. VI–VII), from **「開學了」** (School Starts, p. 1) to **「我要去投票」** (I'm Going to Cast My Vote, p. 281). Topics include school, shopping, climate and festivals, Taiwan hospitality, popular culture, agriculture, family, education, online shopping, health care, Taiwan history, and elections. The series introduction says Volume 3 introduces beginning-level written language and discourse alongside extended dialogues (printed p. V).
