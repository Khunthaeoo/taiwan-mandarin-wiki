# Evidence card ACCC-05 — *A Course in Contemporary Chinese*, Vol. 5

**Source record:** [ACCC-05](../source-register.md)

**PDF title/author metadata:** `當代中文課程5 – 課本`; author field `鄧守信`. The foreword identifies the Mandarin Training Center at National Taiwan Normal University as the series organization and names 鄧守信 as chief editor (printed p. II).  
**Publisher note:** The foreword thanks 聯經出版事業股份有限公司 for publishing assistance (printed p. II); the formal imprint is not confirmed. The foreword is dated May 4, 2018 (printed p. II), not treated as a verified publication year.

## Structure and relevant content

The contents list 10 lessons (printed pp. VI–VII), from **「言論自由的界線」** (The Limits of Free Speech, p. 1) to **「同性婚姻合法化」** (Legalizing Same Sex Marriage, p. 211). Topics include human rights, technology, economics, culture, environment, and other contemporary controversies. The volume description reports 926 vocabulary items and 86 grammar points and describes the readings as roughly 500–1,000 characters, with argumentation, reading comprehension, language practice, and paragraph-expression activities (printed p. V). Chinese–English and English–Chinese vocabulary indexes begin at printed pp. 231 and 244; a simplified-character reference begins at p. 262 (contents, p. VII).

## Wiki-level support

The volume description characterizes its difficulty as approximately CEFR B2–C1 and ACTFL Advanced Mid–Superior (printed p. V), and says it is suitable for learners who have studied the first four volumes or have over 600 study hours and a 3,000-word vocabulary. This is the book's own description; it is not an independent level evaluation or a mapping to the project's A1, A2, B1, B2, and C1 levels. No TOCFL equivalence is stated.

## Limitations and gaps

- Explanations use English, not Burmese.
- Pinyin, sounds, and tones are discussed in the introduction, but systematic Zhuyin teaching and practice are not established.
- Listening exercises and answer references are described as part of separate workbook/teacher materials; those materials were not supplied.
- The volume description says its position articles were synthesized and rewritten from major arguments on each issue. Their claims and cited materials need independent verification before Wiki use.
- Topics are debate-oriented and do not provide comprehensive everyday Taiwan situations such as transport, shopping, or ordering food.
- This evidence card emphasizes the volume description, contents, lesson highlights, and selected early lesson pages; it is not a full lesson-by-lesson review.
