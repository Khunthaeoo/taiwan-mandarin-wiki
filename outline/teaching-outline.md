# Teaching outline — A1 Pronunciation: Taiwan Mandarin Basics

## Scope

One beginner lesson in `wiki/A1/Pronunciation/`. A1 is this Wiki's organizing level; no official A1 assignment is claimed for any individual source.

## Approved sequence

1. Learning objectives: distinguish Zhuyin and Hanyu Pinyin, identify four tones and neutral tone, and read short examples.
2. Introduce basic Mandarin syllable components and tone awareness.
3. Present Zhuyin and Pinyin as different notation systems for pronunciation; use MOE Zhuyin reference and textbook Pinyin evidence.
4. Explain the four tones and neutral tone using original short practice syllables; note that connected-speech tone changes exist without teaching the full system.
5. Add sourced beginner words/phrases with clearly labeled original pronunciation annotations and a short original dialogue.
6. Include simple pronunciation drills, a short quiz with key, and self-assessment.
7. Provide Traditional Chinese and original, simple Burmese explanations throughout.

## Source plan

- Primary notation reference: ACCC-16, MOE *國語注音符號手冊*, printed pp. 4–7.
- Primary verified beginner textbook: ACCC-01, *A Course in Contemporary Chinese*, Vol. 1, printed pp. X–XII, XIX–XX, and sourced phrase examples pp. 2 and 85. Do not claim Vol. 1 is officially A1.
- Narrow supplementary context: ACCC-17 NTU course outline CH2–CH5; ACCC-14 phrasebook p. 11 for its documented 不 tone-change note; ACCC-18 Huayu101 chapter 1 for displayed Pinyin and embedded-audio controls.
- Do not use the unverified local MP3s. Do not copy protected content or claim source audio was checked.
