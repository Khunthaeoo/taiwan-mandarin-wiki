# Project instructions

- Follow the Graph workflow in `.agents/workflows/taiwan-mandarin-wiki.md`; use small, bounded correction loops.
- Follow **Discuss first, generate later**. Obtain the user's approval of the teaching outline before drafting full Wiki lessons or slides.
- Organize content at A1, A2, B1, B2, and C1 across all six sections. These are project levels, not asserted TOCFL equivalents.
- Use Taiwan Mandarin, Traditional Chinese, Burmese explanations, Zhuyin, and Pinyin. Include goals, examples, exercises, dialogues, quizzes, answer keys, and self-assessment.
- Use at least five reliable sources, prioritizing Taiwan government and university resources. Record exact source locations and evidence cards; preserve and explain relevant source differences.
- Have an independent reviewer check content against original sources and evidence cards.
- Run the checks in `workflow/quality-checklists.md`. Report unresolved issues when a loop reaches three correction rounds; do not mark failed work as passed.
- Never fabricate sources, evidence, review findings, test results, or execution records. Keep stage inputs and outputs separate.
