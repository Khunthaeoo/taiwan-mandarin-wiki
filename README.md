# Taiwan Mandarin Learning Wiki

## Purpose and target learners

This project is a source-backed learning Wiki for people studying Mandarin as used in Taiwan. It is designed to support learners with Traditional Chinese, Zhuyin, Hanyu Pinyin, English, and Burmese explanations. The current lessons are small A1 pilots; they are not a complete course.

## Planned learning structure

The planned Wiki has five project levels: **A1, A2, B1, B2, and C1**. These are the project's own levels and are not claimed to match CEFR, TOCFL, or TBCL levels.

The six planned learning sections are:

1. Pronunciation
2. Vocabulary
3. Grammar
4. Listening and Speaking
5. Reading and Writing
6. Taiwan Culture and Daily Life

Full coverage across all levels and sections is planned, but has not been completed.

- The two A1 lessons are pilots. The complete A1–C1 Wiki is not finished.

- The approval record covers only the Pronunciation pilot. No approval is recorded for the Vocabulary pilot, and approval before creating the HTML slides is not documented.
- The saved HTML evidence is limited to the manual test report and six screenshots. The user reports that the presentation worked in Edge, but the screenshots do not document all interactions, console checks, or responsive-size checks.
